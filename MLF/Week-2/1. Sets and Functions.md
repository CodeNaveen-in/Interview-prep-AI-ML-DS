# Vector Space
- If $V$ is a vector space.
	- $u \in V, u \in V$
	- $\alpha , \beta \in \mathbb{R}$
	- $\alpha \cdot u + \beta \cdot v \in V$
- $\mathbb{R}^d$ is a vector space.
- $x \cdot y = x^Ty = \sum^{d}_{i=1} x_iy_i$ (dot product)
- $|| x ||^2 = x \cdot x = x^Tx = \sum^{d}_{i=1} x_i^2$
- x & y are penpendicular / orthogonal
	- $x \cdot y = x^Ty = \sum x_iy_i = 0$
