# Higher Order Approximations
- Linear approx : $f(x) \approx f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^*)$
- Quadratic approx : $f(x) \approx f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^*) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot (x^*) (x-x^*)^2$
- e.g : $f(x) = x^2$
	- $f'(x) = 2x$
	- $f''(x) = 2$
	- $x^2 \approx (x^*)^2 + 2x^*(x-x^*) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot (x-x^*)^2$
	- $x^2$
	- Approx $e^x \text{ around } x^* = 0$
	- $e^x \approx e^0 + e^0(x-x^*) + e^0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot (x-x^*)$
	- $= 1+x+\frac{x^2}{2}$
- Which is closest to $(1.1)^7$?
	- $(a) 1.7 \\(b)1.9 \\(c)2.1 \\(d)2.3$
	- $f(x) = (1+7)^7, f'(x) = 7(1+x)^2, f''(x) = 42(1+x)^5$
	- $f'(0) = 7, f''(0) = 42$
	- $f(0.1) \approx 1 + 7(0.1) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 42 (0.01)$
	- 1.91
# Product Rule
- $f(x) = g(x) \cdot h(x)$
- $f'(x) = ?, x^* = 0$
- $f(x) \approx (g(0) + xg'[0]) \cdot (h(0) + xh'[0])$
- $g(0)h(0) + x[g'(0)h(0) + h'(0)g(0)] + x^2g'(0)h'(0)$
- $L_x[f] = f(0) + xf'(0)$
- $f'(0) = g'(0)h(0) + h'(0)g(0)$

# Maxima, Minima and Saddle point
- $L_{x^x}[f] = f(x^*) + f'(x^*) (x-x^*)$
- $f'(x^*) = 0 <=> x^* \text{ is a critical point of } f$
- $L_{x^*}[f] = 7$
- **Minima** : It is the lowest point of the graph
- **Maxima** : It is the maximum point of the graph
- **Saddle point** : It is the point around which graph changes directions
