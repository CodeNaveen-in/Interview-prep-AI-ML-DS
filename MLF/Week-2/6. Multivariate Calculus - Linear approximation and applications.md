# Gradients and Linear Approximations
- $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$
	- $f(x) \approx f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^*)$
	- $v \in \mathbb{R}^d, x \in \mathbb{R}^d$
	- $f(x) \approx f(v) + \nabla f(v)^T(x-v)$
		- $= f(v) + \sum^{d}_{i=1} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i} (v) \cdot (x_i - v_i)$
- $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$
	- $f(y_1, v_2) \approx f(v_1, v_2) + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1} (v) \cdot (y_1 - v_1)$
	- $f(y_1, v_2) - f(v_1, v_2) \approx \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(v)(y_1 - v_1)$
	- $f(y_1, v_2) - f(v_1, v_2) \approx \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}(v)(y_2 - v_2)$
	- $f(y_1, y_2) - f(v_1, v_2) \approx \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(v)(y_1 - v_1) + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2} (v) (y_2-v_2)$
	- $f(y_1, y_2) \approx f(v_1, v_2) + \nabla f(v)^T (y-v)$
- $f(x_1, x_2) = x_1^2 + x_2^2$

# Directional Directive
- $D_u[f](v) = \lim_{\alpha \to 0} \frac{f(v+\alpha u)}{\alpha}$
- $\lim_{\alpha \to 0} \frac{f(v) + \nabla f(u)^T \alpha u - f(v)}{\alpha}$
- $\nabla f(v)^T u$
# Cauchy - Schwarz Inequality
- $a_1, a_2, \dots , a_d$
- $b_1, b_2, \dots , b_d$
- $- ||a|| \cdot ||b|| \leq a^Tb \leq ||a|| ||b||$
	- $a = \alpha b$
# Direction of Steepest Ascent
- Find a direction $u$, that maximises the rate of change of $f$ as you move from $v$ along $u$.
	- Maximise $D_u[f](v)$
- Find $u \in \mathbb{R}^d$ , $||u|| = 1$ and which maximises
	- $D_u[f](v)$
	- $\nabla f(v)^Tu$
	- $u = \alpha \cdot \nabla f (u)$
# Descent Directions
- $f : \mathbb{d} \to \mathbb{R}$
- $v \in \mathbb{d}$
- What are the valid directions, such that for what values of u : 
	- $D_u[f](v) < 0$
	- $\nabla f(v)^Tu < 0$
- Descent directions = { $u \in \mathbb{R}^d : \nabla f(v)^Tu < 0$ }
