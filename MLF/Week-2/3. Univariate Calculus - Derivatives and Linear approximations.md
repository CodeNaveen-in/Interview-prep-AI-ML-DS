- Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a different function.
$$f'(x^*) = \lim_{x \to x^*} \frac{f(x) - f(x^*)}{x - x^*}$$
$$f'(x^*) \approx \frac{f(x) - f(x^*)}{x - x^*}$$
$$f(x) \approx {f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^*)}$$
$$ \text{where } L_{x^*} [f](x) = f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^*)$$
$$ f(x) \approx L_{x^*} [f] (x)$$

- $L_{x^*}[f] = f(x^*) + f'(x^*)(x-x^x)$
	- $(1)^2+2(x-1)$
	- $1+2(x-1)$
	- $2x-1$
