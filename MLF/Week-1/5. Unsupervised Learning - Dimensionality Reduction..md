# Dimensionality Reduction
- example : Represent a million gene expression levels of a million people, using just 100 numbers per person
- Dimensionality reduction : compression and simplification.
- Data : { $x^1, x^2, ....., x^n$ }
- $x^i \in \mathbb{R}^d$
- Encoder = $f: \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^{d'}$
- Decoder = $g: \mathbb{R}^{d'} \to \mathbb{R}^d$
- Goal : $g(f(x^i)) \approx x^i$
- Loss $= \frac{1}{n} \sum{n}{i=1} || g(f(x^i)) - x^i ||^2$
